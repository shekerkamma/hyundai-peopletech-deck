## Script 1: K-Beauty Glass Skin (Skincare Affiliate)

**Avatar:** Mei
**Hook:** Spicy (close-up tilt reveal)
**Setting:** Bathroom
**Product:** Dr. Melaxin Collagen Boost Set

```
SCRIPT — 15s TikTok/Reel

[0-3s] HOOK: Extreme close-up of Mei's collarbone. Camera slowly tilts up to reveal her glowing glass skin. No words yet — let the skin speak.

[3-7s] REVEAL: Camera pulls to selfie framing. Mei holds up the Dr. Melaxin box with a knowing smile. Bathroom mirror and skincare products visible behind her.

[7-12s] DEMO: Quick cuts of Mei applying serum — dropper to cheek, patting in, showing the glow under bathroom vanity light.

[12-15s] CTA: Mei looks at camera. On-screen text: "Link in bio — $60 for glass skin" with product tag.
```

**CLI generation (when credits available):**
```bash
# Generate with Marketing Studio video (avatar + hook + setting)
higgsfield generate create marketing_studio_video \
  --prompt "K-beauty skincare review. Mei applies collagen serum in bathroom, shows glass skin result. Authentic UGC feel." \
  --aspect_ratio 9:16 --wait
```
