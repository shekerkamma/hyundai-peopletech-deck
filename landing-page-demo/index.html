Brewline strips away complexity to leave only what matters — temperature precision, pressure consistency, and the ritual of making something beautiful.

Precision Grind

Conical ceramic burr grinder with 40 micro-adjustable settings. From Turkish fine to French press coarse — dial in your exact preference.

9-Bar Pressure

Commercial-grade extraction at precisely 9 bars of pressure. PID-controlled temperature holds within ±0.5°C for shot-to-shot consistency.

Articulating steam wand with dry steam technology. Microfoam in under 30 seconds — silky enough for latte art, textured enough for cappuccinos.
