```
Create hooks in these categories:
- 10 "curiosity gap" hooks (make them NEED to watch)
- 5 "controversial take" hooks (spark debate/comments)
- 5 "relatable pain point" hooks (they feel seen)
- 5 "transformation/before-after" hooks

- Each hook must be under 10 words (first 2 seconds of video)

- Mark which platform each hook works best on (TikTok/Reels/Pinterest)

```

```
For each of the 30 days, provide:
- Day number and day of week (starting Monday)
- Content type (transformation, ASMR restock, product review, routine, haul, comparison, etc.)

- Which product(s) to feature
- Hook to use (from the 30 hooks above)
- Platform priority (which platform to post first)
- Best posting time
- CTA type (link in bio, comment "LINK", Amazon storefront, etc.)

- Include 2-3 "non-promotional" value posts per week (tips, hacks, no product push)
- Weekends should have the highest-effort content (transformation reveals)
- Include 1 "trending audio" prompt per week
- Week 1 should be simpler (building confidence), Week 4 should be more advanced
```
