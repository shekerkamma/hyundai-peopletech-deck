# Prompt 3: UGC-Style Product Video (Higgsfield Seedance 2.0)

> **How to use:** Copy the prompt below into claude.ai web (where Higgsfield MCP is connected).
> Claude will generate the video via Higgsfield Seedance 2.0.

---

## Ready-to-Paste Prompt

```
Using Higgsfield Seedance 2.0, generate a realistic UGC-style product video for a HOME ORGANIZATION affiliate marketing campaign.

PRODUCT: Vtopmart Clear Pantry Storage Containers (Amazon)
NICHE: Home Organization & Aesthetic Living
TARGET PLATFORM: TikTok / Instagram Reels (vertical 9:16)

VIDEO CONCEPT:
Create a 5-8 second UGC-style video of a woman in her late 20s in a bright, modern kitchen. She's performing a satisfying pantry restock — pouring dry pasta from a bag into a clear airtight container, with a soft smile of satisfaction. The pantry behind her is beautifully organized with matching clear containers, soft natural lighting coming from a window to the left.

VISUAL STYLE:
- Warm, natural lighting (golden hour kitchen feel)
- Clean, modern kitchen aesthetic (white marble counters, light wood shelves)
- Camera angle: slightly overhead, capturing hands and containers
- UGC feel — slightly imperfect, authentic, not overly produced
- The clear containers should be the visual hero
- Soft depth of field focusing on the pouring action

MOOD: Satisfying, calming, aspirational — the "that girl" aesthetic
COLORS: Neutral tones (whites, creams, light wood) with pops of color from the food items

This should feel like something a real person filmed on their phone during a Sunday kitchen organizing session, not a polished commercial.
```
