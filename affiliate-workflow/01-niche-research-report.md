### Beginner Strategy
1. **Start with:** Stanley, LED lights, mini vacuum, drawer organizers ($30-$60 per product)
2. **Scale to:** Caraway, Dyson/Shark, Cloud Couch dupes ($25-$240 per conversion)

### Platform Allocation
- **Pinterest:** Long-tail engine (6-12 month content lifespan). Evergreen product roundups.
- **TikTok:** Growth engine. Trend-riding, transformation reveals.
- **Instagram Reels:** Conversion engine. Higher purchasing intent audience.

### Content Cadence
- 1 video/day on TikTok + Reels (same content repurposed)
- 3-5 pins/day on Pinterest
- Weekly Amazon storefront update

### Commission Structure
- Amazon Associates: 3-4% ($0.50-$80 per sale depending on product)
- Direct brand programs: 8-20% (Caraway $30-$70, Our Place $13-$25)
- LTK/ShopMy: Higher commissions with built-in shopping infrastructure
