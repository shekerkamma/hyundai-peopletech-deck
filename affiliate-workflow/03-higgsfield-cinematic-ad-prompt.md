# Prompt 4: Cinematic Ad Video (Higgsfield Seedance 2.0)

> **How to use:** Copy the prompt below into claude.ai web (where Higgsfield MCP is connected).
> Claude will generate the video via Higgsfield Seedance 2.0.

---

## Ready-to-Paste Prompt

```
Using Higgsfield Seedance 2.0, generate a cinematic-quality short advertisement video for a home organization product campaign.

PRODUCT: Caraway Home Cookware Set (premium non-toxic ceramic cookware)
NICHE: Home Organization & Aesthetic Living
TARGET PLATFORM: Pinterest Video Pin / Instagram Reels (vertical 9:16)

VIDEO CONCEPT:
A slow, cinematic reveal of a pristine kitchen. Camera glides across a marble countertop where a Caraway cookware set is artfully displayed — matching sage green pans, pots, and lids on a wooden magnetic rack. Steam rises gently from a pan on the stove. A hand reaches in to stir with a wooden spoon. The lighting is dramatic — warm side-lighting with soft shadows.

VISUAL STYLE:
- Cinematic color grading (warm, slightly desaturated, film-like)
- Slow, deliberate camera movement (dolly or slider feel)
- Shallow depth of field — product in focus, background softly blurred
- Premium commercial aesthetic — this should feel like a brand ad
- Rich textures: ceramic cookware surface, wood grain, marble veining
- Warm tungsten lighting from the side, creating depth and dimension

COLOR PALETTE: Sage green (cookware), warm white marble, light oak wood, copper/brass accents
MOOD: Elevated, premium, aspirational — "your dream kitchen"
DURATION: 5-8 seconds

This should feel like a high-end brand commercial, NOT UGC. Think Williams-Sonoma or Architectural Digest aesthetic.
```
