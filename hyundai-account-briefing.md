## What They're Doing Right Now (2026)

### 1. NVIDIA AI Factory
Building an NVIDIA Blackwell-based AI supercomputer to power in-vehicle AI, autonomous driving, **smart factories**, and robotics. This is their core AI infrastructure play — they're investing in foundation, not point solutions.

### 2. Software-Defined Factory (SDF)
Their flagship manufacturing strategy. A single production line can switch between **10 different vehicle models** (hybrid, BEV, extended-range) in weeks. Data and software drive the entire production process. This is their competitive moat in manufacturing agility.

### 3. AI Robotics (CES 2026)
Unveiled next-generation Atlas robots for human-robot collaboration in factories. Theme: "Partnering Human Progress." Commercializing robotic co-workers for plant operations.

### 4. Digital Twin (NVIDIA Omniverse)
Using Omniverse Enterprise for factory digital twins — virtual replicas for precision control, software/hardware-in-the-loop validation, discrete event simulation, and virtual commissioning.

### 5. KRW 9 Trillion Investment
~$6.7B investment starting 2026 to build a cutting-edge industrial complex. Also: Saudi Arabia plant launching Q4 2026 with 50K unit capacity and next-gen robotics.

### 6. Quality Leadership
Tied for #1 in J.D. Power 2025 U.S. Initial Quality Study (second consecutive year). Ulsan Plant 5 and Kia AutoLand Gwangju Plant 1 ranked 2nd in Asia-Pacific assembly quality.

## Pain Points (Where PeopleTech Fits)

### Pain 1: Scaling SDF Across Global Plants
The Software-Defined Factory works at pilot scale. Scaling it to 15+ global plants with consistent quality while switching between 10 model variants requires AI-powered orchestration that doesn't exist in their current stack.

### Pain 2: Predictive Quality Across Model Variants
They're #1 in quality today, but switching 10 models on one line creates exponentially more quality failure modes. Traditional SPC can't keep up — they need AI-driven anomaly detection that adapts to each model variant in real-time.

### Pain 3: Digital Twin → Real-Time Decision Bridge
They have digital twins (Omniverse), but the gap between simulation and real-time production decisions is manual. AI bridging — feeding live sensor data back into the twin and closing the loop with automated adjustments — is the missing piece.

### Pain 4: Human-Robot Collaboration Safety & Optimization
Atlas robots are being deployed, but optimizing human-robot task allocation in real-time (not just pre-programmed sequences) requires AI that understands both robot capabilities and human worker states.

### Pain 5: New Plant Ramp-Up Speed
Saudi Arabia plant launching Q4 2026. Getting a greenfield plant to full quality/throughput in months instead of years is a massive competitive advantage — AI-accelerated ramp-up is the key enabler.
