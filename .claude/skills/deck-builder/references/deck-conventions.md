# Deck Conventions — Reference

## Slide structure

- **Title slide**: Company logo, deck title, date, "Confidential" footer
- **Section dividers**: Dark background, section title, 1-line subtitle
- **Content slides**: Title + 3-4 bullet points max, supporting visual/diagram
- **Architecture slides**: Full-bleed diagram with callout annotations
- **ROI slides**: Before/after metrics, sourced from ROI Calculator

## Color palette

- Primary: Hyundai Blue (#002C5F)
- Accent: PeopleTech Orange (#FF6B35)
- Background: White (#FFFFFF) or Dark (#1A1A2E)
- Text: Dark Gray (#333333) on light, White on dark

## Typography

- Titles: 28-32pt bold
- Body: 18-22pt regular
- Footnotes: 12pt italic
