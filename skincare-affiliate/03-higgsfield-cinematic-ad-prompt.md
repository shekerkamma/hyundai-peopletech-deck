## Primary Prompt — "Liquid Gold"

```
Using Higgsfield Seedance 2.0, generate a cinematic product advertisement video for a premium K-Beauty skincare campaign.

PRODUCT: Medicube Glass Glow Skincare Set
NICHE: K-Beauty / Premium Skincare
TARGET PLATFORM: TikTok / Instagram Reels (vertical 9:16)

VIDEO CONCEPT:
A cinematic beauty ad opening on a tight shot of golden serum droplets falling in slow motion onto a glass surface, catching dramatic side-light. Camera pulls back to reveal an elegant arrangement of Medicube skincare bottles on a marble pedestal, surrounded by soft golden bokeh. A woman's hand reaches in and lifts one bottle — the camera follows her hand to her face as she applies the serum, skin catching the light in a luminous, editorial glow.

CINEMATIC DIRECTION:
- Opening: Macro shot of serum drops hitting glass (slow motion feel)
- Mid: Product hero shot — bottles arranged on marble, dramatic side-lighting
- Close: Model application — serum on cheek, skin catching golden light
- Camera movement: Slow dolly push-in, smooth and deliberate
- Lighting: Single dramatic key light from camera-left, warm golden tone
- Color grade: Rich, warm gold tones with deep shadows — luxury feel
- Depth of field: Extremely shallow, f/1.4 look — creamy bokeh background

MOOD: Premium, luxurious, editorial — Vogue Beauty meets K-beauty
COLORS: Gold, warm amber, deep shadows, translucent serum highlights
```

**Higgsfield settings:** model `seedance_2_0`, aspect_ratio `9:16`, duration `5`
**Estimated cost:** ~23 credits
