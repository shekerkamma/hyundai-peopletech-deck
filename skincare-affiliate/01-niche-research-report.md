## Content Formats That Convert

1. **"Glass Skin" transformation** — before (bare) → after (glowing) in 15 seconds
2. **Texture test** — close-up of serum/cream absorption on skin
3. **Routine walkthrough** — step 1-2-3 with product tags
4. **"Week 1 vs Week 4"** — progress diary format
5. **"Under $100 skincare haul"** — multiple products, price tags on screen
6. **ASMR application** — satisfying sounds + visuals, no voiceover needed
