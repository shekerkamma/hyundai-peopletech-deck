## 5. Multi-Platform Repurposing Guide

### One Video → 7 Pieces of Content

| # | Platform | Format | What to Change |
|---|----------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | **TikTok** | Original shoppable video | Tag products in TikTok Shop |
| 2 | **Instagram Reels** | Same video, different caption | Add hashtag strategy, link in bio CTA |
| 3 | **Instagram Stories** | 15s clip + poll sticker | "Have you tried K-beauty? Yes/Not yet" |
| 4 | **Pinterest Video Pin** | Same video, SEO title + description | Keyword-rich pin description |
| 5 | **Pinterest Idea Pin** | Screenshots from video as slides | Add text overlay with product names |
| 6 | **YouTube Shorts** | Same video, different thumbnail | Custom thumbnail with before/after |
| 7 | **Instagram Carousel** | Still frames from video + text | Swipeable routine walkthrough |

### Repurposing Rules
- **TikTok first** — film and post here for freshest algorithm boost
- **Remove watermark** before cross-posting (use SnapTik or save original)
- **Change first 2 seconds** for each platform — algorithms penalize exact duplicates
- **Captions differ per platform** — TikTok (short + hooks), Pinterest (SEO-heavy), Instagram (mid-length + hashtags)
- **Repost winners** 2-3 times with new hooks — proven content deserves more reach
