## Primary Prompt — "Glass Skin Routine"

```
Using Higgsfield Seedance 2.0, generate a realistic UGC-style skincare video for a K-BEAUTY affiliate marketing campaign.

PRODUCT: Dr. Melaxin Gifted Collagen Boost Set
NICHE: K-Beauty Skincare / Glass Skin
TARGET PLATFORM: TikTok / Instagram Reels (vertical 9:16)

VIDEO CONCEPT:
Create a 5-second UGC-style video of a woman in her mid-20s in a bright, clean bathroom. She's applying a glowing serum from a dropper onto her cheek, gently patting it in with her fingertips. Her skin visibly catches the light — dewy, glass-like finish. The elegant Korean skincare bottles are arranged neatly on the counter beside her. Soft morning light from a frosted window.

VISUAL STYLE:
- Soft, diffused natural lighting (clean bathroom morning light)
- Close-up angle on face and hands, slightly tilted
- Dewy skin texture is the hero — light catching the glow
- Clean white/cream bathroom background, minimal clutter
- UGC authenticity — slight camera movement, not studio-perfect
- Product bottles visible but not centered (organic placement)

MOOD: Fresh, satisfying, aspirational — "glass skin goals"
COLORS: Soft whites, cream, translucent golden serum, dewy skin highlights
```

**Higgsfield settings:** model `seedance_2_0`, aspect_ratio `9:16`, duration `5`
**Estimated cost:** ~23 credits
