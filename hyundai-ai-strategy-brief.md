# AI Strategy Brief — Hyundai Motor Group x PeopleTech

**One-page executive brief for pre-sales positioning**

---

## The Opportunity

Hyundai Motor Group is investing $6.7B+ in next-generation manufacturing and has publicly committed to Software-Defined Factories, NVIDIA AI infrastructure, and AI robotics. They have the vision and budget. What they lack is the **AI operations layer** that connects their factory floor data to real-time decisions — the software between the digital twin and the production line.

**PeopleTech fills the gap between Hyundai's infrastructure investments and operational AI outcomes.**

---

## Market Context

- Global smart manufacturing market: $950B by 2030 (CAGR 14.9%)
- Automotive AI in manufacturing: $12.4B by 2028
- Hyundai's competitors (Toyota, VW, Tesla) are all deploying factory AI — this is table stakes, not differentiation
- The differentiator is **speed of deployment** and **multi-model flexibility** — exactly what SDF demands

## Three Proposals Worth Making

### Proposal 1: Predictive Quality for SDF (Quick Win — 3 months)
Deploy AI-driven quality prediction on one SDF line handling 3+ model variants. Demonstrate measurable defect reduction vs. traditional SPC.
- **ROI hook:** Every 1% defect reduction = ~$13M saved annually (at $132B revenue, warranty costs are ~1% of revenue)
- **Entry point:** Ulsan Plant 5 (already top-ranked in quality — they'll want to stay #1)

### Proposal 2: Greenfield Ramp-Up Accelerator (Timely — Saudi plant Q4 2026)
AI-driven ramp-up optimization for the new Saudi Arabia plant. Compress time-to-full-quality from 12-18 months to 4-6 months.
- **ROI hook:** Every month of faster ramp = ~50K units × average margin = significant revenue acceleration
- **Entry point:** Greenfield = no legacy systems, clean deployment

### Proposal 3: Digital Twin Closed-Loop (Strategic — 12 months)
Bridge the gap between Omniverse digital twins and live production. Automated feedback loop: sensor data → twin validation → production adjustment → outcome measurement.
- **ROI hook:** Closes the "last mile" of their biggest infrastructure investment
- **Entry point:** Eunsook Jin (ICT/Digital) — her team owns Omniverse integration
