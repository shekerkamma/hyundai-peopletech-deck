# Conversation Prep: Hyundai Motor Group Pitch Meeting

**With:** Hyundai Motor Group manufacturing/digital leadership
**About:** PeopleTech AI Plant Operations platform
**Your Goal:** Secure a discovery workshop and 90-day pilot at Ulsan Plant 5
**Their Likely Goal:** Understand if PeopleTech can accelerate their SDF rollout without disrupting existing NVIDIA/Omniverse investments

---

## Before You Start

**Mindset:** This is not a cold pitch. Hyundai has already publicly committed to AI in manufacturing — they're investing $6.7B. You're not selling them on AI. You're showing them you understand their specific architecture (SDF) better than any other vendor and can deliver outcomes faster.

**Timing:** Best after their Q2 board review when 2026 investment plans are confirmed. Avoid months when Saudi plant prep dominates attention (Oct-Dec 2026).

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't lead with generic AI capabilities. They've heard it from Siemens, Rockwell, and C3.ai.
- Don't position against NVIDIA or Omniverse. Those are sunk investments. Position as complementary.

---

## The Opening

**Option 1 (Direct):**
"Your Software-Defined Factory can switch between 10 models on a single line. That's a manufacturing breakthrough — but it also creates 10x the quality failure modes. We've built the AI layer that keeps quality at J.D. Power #1 while you scale SDF globally."

**Option 2 (Softer):**
"We've been studying your SDF architecture closely. The flexibility is impressive — and we think we can help solve the hardest part of scaling it: maintaining consistent quality across model variants without slowing down the line."

**Option 3 (Question-Led):**
"When you switch between model variants on an SDF line, how does your quality team currently adapt their inspection parameters? We've found that's where most multi-model lines hit a wall — and we've built something specifically for that problem."

---

## Key Points to Make

1. **"We're not another IoT platform."** — "You already have NVIDIA Omniverse for digital twins, SAP for ERP, and your own sensor infrastructure. We sit between all of that and the production decisions your line managers make. We're the AI decision layer, not another data platform."

2. **"SDF is your competitive advantage — we protect it."** — "No other automaker can switch 10 models on one line. But that flexibility means traditional quality systems break down. Our AI adapts quality parameters in real-time per model variant. We're the only vendor that's built specifically for multi-model flexible manufacturing."

3. **"The Saudi plant is the perfect starting point."** — "Greenfield = no legacy systems, no integration battles. We can pre-deploy our AI layer before the line goes live, so you hit full quality from day one instead of spending 12-18 months ramping up."

4. **"Here's what 1% defect reduction is worth to you."** — "At your revenue scale, warranty and recall costs run approximately $1.3B annually. A 1% improvement in defect detection is worth roughly $13M per year. Our Ulsan pilot would target 3-5% improvement in the first 90 days."

5. **"We complement, not compete, with your existing stack."** — "NVIDIA gives you the compute. Omniverse gives you the twin. We give you the closed loop — live sensor data validated against the twin, anomaly flagged, production adjusted, outcome measured. All automated."

## Likely Pushback & How to Respond

### If they say: "We're already working with Siemens/Rockwell on this."
**Respond with:** "That makes sense — they're strong on connectivity and OT integration. What we've found is that their AI capabilities are retrofitted onto platforms designed for monitoring, not decision-making. We're AI-native. Happy to run a head-to-head on a single line to show the difference in detection speed and accuracy."

### If they say: "We want to build this capability in-house with our NVIDIA investment."
**Respond with:** "Your NVIDIA AI factory is the right foundation for training models. What takes time is the manufacturing domain expertise — encoding the knowledge of what a quality deviation looks like across 10 model variants on an SDF line. That's 15 years of our engineering. We can save your team 2-3 years of iteration."

### If they say: "The budget is already allocated for 2026."
**Respond with:** "Understood. A pilot at Ulsan Plant 5 is under $1M — well within discretionary range for Juncheul Jung's manufacturing org. And the Saudi plant scoping could fall under the new plant capex budget, not the AI line item. We're flexible on how this gets structured."

### If they say: "How do we know this works at our scale?"
**Respond with:** "Fair question. We'd propose a 90-day proof of value at Ulsan Plant 5 on 3 model variants with hard success metrics agreed upfront: defect detection rate, false positive rate, and time-to-detection. If we don't hit the targets, you walk away with the data and learnings for free."

### If they get technical and ask about integration:
**Respond with:** "We integrate via standard OPC-UA from your PLCs and sensor layer, REST APIs to Omniverse for twin data, and MQTT for real-time streaming. Deployment is containerized — runs on your NVIDIA infrastructure or ours. No rip-and-replace required."

### If they shut down or defer:
**Respond with:** "No pressure at all. Would it be useful if we put together a technical white paper showing exactly how our AI layer would integrate with your SDF architecture? That way your team can evaluate it independently before we schedule anything."

## Your Non-Negotiables

- **Must have:** A face-to-face discovery workshop with the manufacturing team (not just IT procurement)
- **Ideal outcome:** Agreement to a 90-day pilot at Ulsan Plant 5 with defined success metrics
- **Walk-away point:** If they only want a generic RFP process with 12+ month timelines — that's a signal they're not ready. Offer the white paper and revisit in 6 months.

---

## How to Close

- **If it goes well:** "Great — let's get a 2-hour discovery workshop on the calendar with your SDF line team at Ulsan. We'll bring our manufacturing AI architect and come prepared with a draft integration map. What does Juncheul-san's calendar look like in the next 3 weeks?"

- **If it's unresolved:** "I appreciate the time today. Let me send over two things: (1) the SDF-specific technical brief, and (2) the ROI model we built for your plant configuration. Can we reconnect in two weeks after your team has reviewed them?"

- **If it goes badly:** "I understand — the timing may not be right. Our door is open. When the Saudi plant ramp-up starts and the pressure to hit quality targets on day one kicks in, that's usually when this conversation becomes urgent. We'll be here."
